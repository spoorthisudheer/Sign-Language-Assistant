Linguistic Note on Sign Language Grammar

Natural Indian Sign Language has its own rich grammatical structure (Topic-Comment order, spatial indexing, facial non-manual markers) which differs fundamentally from English word order. Sequential word cards offer lexical representation for learning and assistive reference; words not in the core vocabulary are systematically broken down into finger-spelled ISL alphabets.
