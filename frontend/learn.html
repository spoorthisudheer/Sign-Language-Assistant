Indian Sign Language (ISL) Standard Clarification

Unlike American Sign Language (ASL) where alphabets are one-handed, ISL alphabets are primarily two-handed gestures. Our curriculum is specifically built to ISL specifications so students learn accurate national accessibility standards.
